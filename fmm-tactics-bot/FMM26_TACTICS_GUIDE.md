## 1. The Black Hawk 2026 V23: top pick overall
[Thread](https://fmmvibe.com/forums/topic/51286-the-black-hawk-2026-v23/) by Black Hawk · 177 replies · 152k views ·
active today. Replies since the update: 12 positive, 0 negative. On 21 Sep one user wrote that it's
*"still the best on this site and I have tried all of them even the new ones."*

**Shape: 4-1-2-3 (DM + 2 central midfielders)**

| Line | Roles |
|------|-------|
| GK | Sweeper Keeper (use a plain Goalkeeper if your keeper has poor Technique/Kicking) |
| Defence | WB, CD, CD, WB |
| DM | Roaming Playmaker |
| Midfield | Box-to-Box Mid, Box-to-Box Mid |
| Attack | Inside Forward (L, right-footed), Pressing Forward, Inside Forward (R, left-footed) |

Version B, for teams that are better on the ball: BPD, BPD in place of the CDs, and a **Deep-Lying Forward** in place of the PF.

- **Shape:** Control · Narrow · Normal tempo · Expressive
- **Defence:** Balanced line (only use High if your team is strong *and* your CBs are quick) · Close down All Over · Normal tackling · Offside trap No · Time wasting No
- **Attack:** Work Into Box + Run At Defence · Short passing · Mixed focus · Short GK distribution
- **Set pieces:** the IFs take corners. Striker attacks the near post, one CB marks the keeper, one CB attacks the far post, WBs and RP stay back. Set edge-of-box free kicks to Shoot. Send 5–6 players into the box on penalties.
- **Key attributes:** CBs need Pace. WBs need Pace and Movement. Keeper needs Technique and Kicking if you use SK.

## Patterns that keep coming up in FMM26
1. **Narrow width + Expressive creative freedom** appears in almost every top tactic.
2. **Close down All Over** with **Offside trap off**. Several authors say defenders don't hold the line properly in this match engine.
3. **Short passing + Mixed focus.** The CPU presses so hard that pure central play gets stuck.
4. **Pace at centre-back is essential.** The FMM26 CPU counter-attacks very quickly. Drop to a Balanced line with weaker teams.
5. **BBMs in midfield and Inside Forwards out wide** are the most common attacking pattern.
6. Results vary a lot, even with the same save: team talks and press answers noticeably swing form.
