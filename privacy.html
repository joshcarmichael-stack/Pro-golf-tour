Privacy Policy

Sunday Pins (the website at sunday-pins.com and the Sunday Pins apps) · Last updated 3 October 2026

The short version

Sunday Pins doesn't ask who you are, has no accounts, shows no ads and doesn't sell or share anything about you. Your careers are saved on your own device.

What's stored on your device

  • Your careers, Hall of Fame, settings and any player names you've changed are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device.
  • They never leave your device. Clearing the app's data or your browser's site data deletes them.
  • If you import or export a names file, that file stays wherever you choose to keep it.

Anonymous visit counts

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page visits and a few game moments (for example "a career started" or "a season finished"). It uses no cookies, doesn't track you across sites, and doesn't store your IP address or anything that identifies you. We use these counts only to see which parts of the game people play.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data. We don't collect any.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

Sunday Pins is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Other websites

The website may link to other sites (for example a support page). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the contact email shown on the Sunday Pins listing in the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
